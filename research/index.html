Current & Recent Projects

Wind Engineering

Deep Learning for Wind Load Prediction

Ongoing

Developing deep neural networks to predict wind pressure distributions on high-rise buildings. This research combines CFD simulations with machine learning to create fast and accurate surrogate models for wind load estimation.

Computational Mechanics

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Structural Analysis

Ongoing

Investigating the use of physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) to solve partial differential equations in structural mechanics. This approach combines data-driven methods with fundamental physics principles.

Computer Vision

Computer Vision for Structural Health Monitoring

Ongoing

Developing automated damage detection systems using deep learning and computer vision techniques. Applications include crack detection, deformation measurement, and structural assessment.
