Wildfire Modeling

FIRELINE: Wildfire Spread and Response

Available

Interactive wildfire simulation, "One Simple Rule," that models fire spread across a landscape under environmental conditions including wind direction and intensity. Shows AI-driven response decisions on a 2D map, with an optional 3D WebGL view, and lets users click any cell to inspect the reasoning behind its decision while tracking elapsed fire hours and burned area in hectares. Built as an educational tool for exploring wildfire dynamics.

Latest: Initial release with interactive fire spread simulation and clickable artificial-intelligence decision inspection.
