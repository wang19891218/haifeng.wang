3D Visualization Features

Interactive Models

Fully interactive 3D models with hover effects, click handlers, and animations

Real-Time Rendering

GPU-accelerated rendering with smooth 60 FPS animations and realistic lighting

Orbit Controls

Intuitive camera controls for exploring the 3D scene from any angle

Shadows & Lighting

Realistic shadows, ambient lighting, and dynamic light sources for depth

Grid & Reference

Reference grid and ground plane for spatial orientation and scale

Responsive Design

Adapts to different screen sizes and devices, works on desktop and mobile

Potential Applications

Structural Visualization

Interactive 3D models of buildings, bridges, and other structures for research presentations and educational purposes. Visualize stress distributions, deformation patterns, and failure modes.

Wind Flow Visualization

Display wind flow patterns around buildings using particle systems or vector fields. Demonstrate CFD simulation results in an interactive, easy-to-understand format.

Data Visualization

Create 3D plots and charts for research data, including scatter plots, surface plots, and time-series animations for presenting complex engineering data.

Educational Tools

Interactive demonstrations for teaching structural mechanics concepts, allowing students to manipulate parameters and see real-time effects on structural behavior.
