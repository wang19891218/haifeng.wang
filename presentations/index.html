AI in Education

Teaching with AI in Civil and Environmental Engineering

Password Protected

Three levels of artificial intelligence in teaching, illustrated by a reflection exercise in CE430 Indeterminate Structures. Introduces CivilAI-Companion, a course assistant that answers from the instructor’s own materials rather than the internet and returns flagged responses for review, and StructAgent, which turns a hand sketch into analysis code. Closes with a comparison of hosted and self-hosted deployment.
